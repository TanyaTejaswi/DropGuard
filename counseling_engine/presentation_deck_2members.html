Data Engineering & Preprocessing Pipeline (Member 1)

UCI Dataset #697 Cohort Isolation and ColumnTransformer feature taxonomy.

18 Numerical Features

StandardScaler ($\mu=0.0, \sigma=1.0$)

Curricular units enrolled/approved/grade (sem 1 & 2), admission grade, age at enrollment, GDP, inflation.

10 Categorical Features

OneHotEncoder (Sparse Dummy Cols)

Course IDs, marital status, nationality, father's/mother's qualifications and occupations.

8 Binary Flags

Passthrough ($0/1$ Booleans)

Debtor status, tuition fees up to date, scholarship holder, gender, daytime/evening attendance.

Filtered Binary Cohort: 3,630 students (1,421 Dropouts / 2,209 Graduates) Exported: 3,630 rows × 233 dimensions
